"Test #04 SOLE Voltage"

Clarify the contribution of users to SOLE Voltage.

PURPOSE

Clarify user contributions to SOLE Voltaje.

Questions

Who wants to contribute?

Why do they want to contribute?

How is the contribution currently produced?

What obstacles (technical and non-technical) do users encounter when trying to contribute?

What non-technical changes can be introduced to incentivize contribution?

The day after, how does participant contribution become a daily habit?

What elements are relevant/important for group interaction with SOLE Voltaje?

Moments

The practical one: Tinkering station and SOLE as a practice

DESCRIPTION

  • What will be done?: Clarify user contribution through their active participation in tinkering workshops and alternative SOLE practices (as opposed to the usual practice under ideal conditions).
  • How will it be done?: Through tinkering activities and alternative SOLEs where participants can test this Station according to their motivation.
  • Why?: If people experience a creative tinkering workshop and alternative SOLEing, they can live and imagine use cases for their own challenges or personal/organizational projects and give us feedback regarding the event’s purpose questions.

Chatbox Station

DESCRIPTION

Could we propose a relationship with a Chatbot beyond inputs and outputs? Could a Chatbot, from now on Chatbox, invite us to discover the internet in a way that not even the Chatbox itself can anticipate?

With this station, we want to invite participants to discover the possibilities of SOLE Voltaje and the tinkering spirit by moving through the space. On the other hand, it is a provocation to reflect on those relationships between humans that machines cannot anticipate.

Phases

Moment 1: Chat Box Path: people will walk along a mat with EIGHT (8) possibilities that SOLE Voltaje has. Depending on where people stand, an infrared sensor with Arduino will produce sounds and effects that invite participants to explore the space in relation to where they are standing.

  1. CONVENE/PEOPLE: Do you want to know tools to convene other people to use the internet in a group?
  2. SPACE: Do you want to find a public space to use the internet?
  3. EQUIPMENT: Do you want to get the technology necessary to connect to the internet?
  4. ELECTRICITY: Do you want to find a solution when electricity fails or is intermittent?
  5. SIGNAL: Do you want to find solutions when the internet signal fails or is intermittent?
  6. EXPERIENCES: Do you want to know about the experiences of communities that are improving their internet connectivity?
  7. OFFLINE TINKERING: Do you want to know how the internet and connectivity work in the place where you are?
  8. QUESTIONS (DATA): Do you want to reflect on our relationship with the internet?

Moment 2: Discover the space: upon choosing an option, the ChatBox and the space indicate to the person that they can go explore the space to discover what interested them. Each moment should have a reflection or story that invites them to read, listen, and learn more.

  1. PEOPLE: explore the space and find the tools to convene other people to use the internet in a group
  2. SPACE: explore the space and find a public space to use the internet
  3. EQUIPMENT: explore the space and get the technology necessary to connect to the internet
  4. ELECTRICITY: explore the space and find a solution when electricity fails or is intermittent
  5. SIGNAL: explore the space and find solutions when the internet signal fails or is intermittent
  6. EXPERIENCES: explore the space and learn about the experiences of communities that are improving their internet connectivity
  7. OFFLINE TINKERING: explore the space and learn how the internet and connectivity work in the place where you are
  8. QUESTIONS (DATA): explore the space and reflect with some questions about our relationship with the internet

Moment 3: Return or continue:

Each moment will have 3 options that will be distributed randomly throughout the space and will enable multiple possibilities to discover and explore Voltaje. Upon finishing an option, the participant can return (or go for the first time) to the ChatBox or continue with other stories of the same option or other options.

Resources

  • Yarn of different colors
  • Post-its
  • Corrugated cardboard (half sheet)

How is feedback received? How does the tinkering spirit emerge according to the experience?

People will leave a little dot on the options they visited throughout the tour. At the end, we will ask why they visited those options and how they felt when visiting them.

ChatTinkering Station

DESCRIPTION

How do we realize that we are not alone in this tinkering business? How do we accompany and support each other in exploring solutions to improve our experience of using the Internet in a group?

The WhatsApp chat is a place for people who are having connectivity challenges or who are building Voltaje solutions to:

  • find a community that helps them get through specific difficulties they may have
  • be able to share/receive new solutions that are not in Voltaje
  • share/receive how in other places they are using solutions that may or may not be in Voltaje (for example, “5 tricks for…” or other content we can find on the internet and see how people react) - watch out at the end, the invitation is to use the internet in a group
  • share/receive interesting information about connectivity challenges and solutions in the world, discoveries, work in other places, initiatives…
  • provide support to those who venture to explore the Voltaje space, and the test, and find themselves lost - helper?

The purpose of the activity is to observe how people participating in the challenges interact with this channel, analyze if it is important and useful for them, and receive feedback on the interaction that develops that day to make the constructions and/or modifications required to fulfill its mission, and provide mutual support to the people who interact with Voltaje and the test.

PHASES

Purpose:

Experience using the WhatsApp chat to share and discuss connectivity solutions. Each challenge lasts 30 minutes and challenges can take place at the same time that participants interact with the rest of the installation activities. There is a group that starts at this station and is in charge of starting challenge 1; after 30 minutes, we notify them so that the next group starts challenge 2 (the same is repeated with 3).

Activities:

The presentation of each challenge is done by Bel through the WhatsApp group.

  • Challenge 1 (30 minutes): The group in charge of the challenge must decide among all the presentation entries which connectivity challenge they find the coolest. The discussion takes place live (between the people interacting in this group) and in the chat, with people who are not at the location. People who are in other stations can also participate in the discussion, but those who facilitate or lead this challenge are those in the starting group. In the chat, it must be clear which connectivity challenge they choose to solve.
  • Challenge 2 (30 minutes): The group in charge of Challenge 2 must find the solution for the challenge proposed in the first challenge, consulting the SOLE Voltaje guide and what they have experienced in the rest of the space. The discussion must be reflected in the chat and the decision of which solution is the most appropriate must be clear to all members of the chat. They can request opinions and experiences from all members. The tinkerers in the chat can stimulate the discussion and can also encourage the exploration of the space in the workshop, or experimentation in the other stations.
  • Challenge 3 (30 minutes): The group in charge picks up the baton from the previous group (they must understand it by reading the chat) and selects the interactions in the chat that they found especially useful or interesting. Then, they must analyze how the structure of the chat (such as message organization, type of responses, use of multimedia) contributed to the understanding and resolution of the problem.

RESOURCES

Prints:

  • Station presentation sign - ok
  • Poster with chat information: QR + station description - ok

Others:

  • Presentation video of the entire experience - draft the script
  • Presentation video of the chat-tinkerers - draft the script

Materials:

buy + intervene · 1 bag of balloons to signal in the space which are the chatting places · string to hang them from the ceiling at different heights · “witness” element - can it be the same clock? set the time for the next cut · analog clock to measure time (nicolas@solecolombia.org)

How is feedback received?

How does the tinkering spirit emerge according to the experience?

  • we will analyze the conversation that will take place in the chat
  • hypothesis: people will be encouraged to collaborate, even if delayed, from the experience of choosing the challenge + finding the most appropriate solution + which are the steps of the conversation that helped the most
  • experience systematization template (mateo@solecolombia.org)

What a Video! Station

DESCRIPTION

In this station, attendees will be able to choose which Voltaje video to watch (Homemade Antenna Technique, Homemade Antenna Hook, Jangala Hook, Fish Game Reflection). The experience will be individual, as the person will put on headphones and watch the video they have chosen.

Phases

  1. Experience watching one or more videos
  2. Feedback based on interaction with the poster and the connection and disconnection mailboxes.

RESOURCES

TV, headphones, station sign, poster print, disconnection mailbox. How is feedback received? How does the tinkering spirit emerge according to the experience?

On a poster, people will be able to mark in a box which video(s) they watched. In another box on the poster, they will be able to mark on a scale of 0-5 how much it motivated them to tinker or to discover the solution proposed in each of the videos they watched.

Materials

2 mailboxes, papers, stickers.
