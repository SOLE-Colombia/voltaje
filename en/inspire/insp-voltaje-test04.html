Test #04 SOLE Voltage

Clarifying how users contribute to SOLE Voltage.

PURPOSE

Clarify user contributions to SOLE Voltaje.

Questions

Who wants to contribute?

Why do they want to contribute?

How is the contribution currently produced?

What obstacles (technical and non-technical) do users encounter when trying to contribute?

What non-technical changes can be introduced to encourage contribution?

The day after, how does participant contribution become a daily habit?

What elements are relevant/important for group interaction with SOLE Voltaje?

Moments

The hands-on one: Tinkering station and SOLE as practice

DESCRIPTION

  • What are we going to do?: Clarify user contribution through active participation in tinkering workshops and alternative SOLE practices (different from the usual practice under ideal conditions).
  • How are we going to do it?: Through tinkering activities and alternative SOLEs where participants can test this Station according to their motivation.
  • Why?: If people experience a creative tinkering workshop and alternative SOLEing, they can live and imagine use cases for their own challenges or personal/organizational projects and give us feedback regarding the event’s purpose questions.

Chatbox Station

DESCRIPTION

Could we propose a relationship with a Chatbot beyond inputs and outputs? Could a Chatbot, from now on Chatbox, invite us to discover the internet in a way that not even the Chatbox itself can anticipate?

With this station, we want to invite participants to discover the possibilities that SOLE Voltaje and the tinkerer spirit have by moving through the space. On the other hand, it is a provocation to reflect on those relationships between humans that machines cannot anticipate.

Phases

Moment 1: Chat Box Path: people will walk along a mat with EIGHT (8) possibilities that SOLE Voltaje has. Depending on where people stand, an infrared sensor with Arduino will produce sounds and effects that invite participants to explore the space with respect to the place where they are standing.

  1. CONVENE/PEOPLE: Do you want to know tools to convene other people to use the internet in a group?
  2. SPACE: Do you want to find a public space to use the internet?
  3. EQUIPMENT: Do you want to get the technology needed to connect to the internet?
  4. ELECTRICITY: Do you want to find a solution when electricity fails or is intermittent?
  5. SIGNAL: Do you want to find solutions when the internet signal fails or is intermittent?
  6. EXPERIENCES: Do you want to know about community experiences that are improving their internet connectivity?
  7. OFFLINE TINKERER: Do you want to know how the internet and connectivity work in the place where you are?
  8. QUESTIONS (DATA): Do you want to reflect on our relationship with the internet?

Moment 2: Discover the space: upon choosing an option, the ChatBox and the space indicate to the person that they can go explore the space to discover what interested them. Each moment should have a reflection or story that invites them to read, listen, and learn more.

  1. PEOPLE: explore the space and find the tools to convene other people to use the internet in a group
  2. SPACE: explore the space and find a public space to use the internet
  3. EQUIPMENT: explore the space and get the technology needed to connect to the internet
  4. ELECTRICITY: explore the space and find a solution when electricity fails or is intermittent
  5. SIGNAL: explore the space and find solutions when the internet signal fails or is intermittent
  6. EXPERIENCES: explore the space and learn about community experiences that are improving their internet connectivity
  7. OFFLINE TINKERER: explore the space and learn how the internet and connectivity work in the place where you are
  8. QUESTIONS (DATA): explore the space and reflect with some questions about our relationship with the internet

Moment 3: Return or continue:

Each moment will have 3 options that will be distributed randomly around the space and will enable multiple possibilities to discover and explore Voltaje. Upon finishing an option, the participant can return (or go for the first time) to the ChatBox or continue with other stories of the same option or other options.

Resources

  • Yarn of different colors
  • Post-its
  • Corrugated cardboard half sheet

How is feedback received in terms of feedback? How does the tinkerer spirit emerge according to the experience?

People will leave a little dot on the options they visited throughout the tour. At the end, we will ask a question about why they visited those options and what they felt when visiting them.

ChatTinkering Station

DESCRIPTION

How do we realize that we are not alone in this tinkering business? How do we accompany and support each other in exploring solutions to improve our experience of using the Internet in a group?

The WhatsApp chat is a place for people who are having connectivity challenges or who are building Voltaje solutions to:

  • find a community that helps them get through the specific difficulties they have
  • be able to share/receive new solutions that are not in Voltaje
  • share/receive how in other places they are using solutions that may or may not be in Voltaje (for example, “5 tricks for…” or other content that we can find on the internet and see how people react) - watch out at the end, the invitation is to use the internet in a group
  • share/receive interesting information about connectivity challenges and solutions in the world, discoveries, work in other places, initiatives…
  • provide support to those who venture to explore the Voltaje space, and the test, and find themselves lost - helper?

The purpose of the activity is to observe how people participating in the challenges interact with this channel, analyze if it is important and useful for them, and receive feedback on the interaction that takes place that day to make the constructions and/or modifications required to fulfill its purpose, and provide support among all to the people who interact with Voltaje and the test.”

PHASES

Purpose:

Experience using the WhatsApp chat to share and discuss connectivity solutions. Each challenge lasts 30 minutes and the challenges can take place at the same time as participants interact with the rest of the installation activities. There is a group that starts at this station and is in charge of starting challenge 1; after 30 minutes, we notify the next group to start challenge 2 (the same is repeated with 3).

Activities:

The presentation of each challenge is done by Bel through the WhatsApp group

  • Challenge 1 (30 minutes): The group in charge of the challenge must decide among all the presentation entries which connectivity challenge they think is the coolest. The discussion happens live (between the people interacting in this group) and in the chat, with the people who are not at the location. People who are at other stations can also participate in the discussion, but those who facilitate or lead this challenge are those in the group that starts. In the chat, it must be clear which connectivity challenge they choose to solve.
  • Challenge 2 (30 minutes): The group that is left in charge of Challenge 2 must find the solution for the challenge proposed in the first challenge, consulting the SOLE Voltaje guide and what they have experienced in the rest of the space. The discussion must be reflected in the chat and the decision of which solution is the most appropriate must be clear to all members of the chat. They can request opinions and experiences from all members. The tinkerers in the chat can stimulate the discussion and can also encourage the exploration of the space in the workshop, or experimentation in the other stations.
  • Challenge 3 (30 minutes): the group in charge picks up the baton from the previous group (must understand it by reading the chat) and selects the interactions in the chat that they found especially useful or interesting. Then, they must analyze how the chat structure (such as message organization, type of responses, use of multimedia) contributed to the understanding and resolution of the problem.”

What a Video! Station

DESCRIPTION

In this station, attendees will be able to choose which Voltaje video to watch (Homemade Antenna Technique, Homemade Antenna Hook, Jangala Hook, Fish Game Reflection). The experience will be individual, as the person will put on headphones and watch the video they have chosen.

Phases

  1. Experience watching one or more videos
  2. Feedback based on interaction with the poster and the connection and disconnection mailboxes.

RESOURCES

Television, headphones, station sign, poster print, disconnection mailbox. How is feedback received in terms of feedback? How does the tinkerer spirit emerge according to the experience?

On a poster, people will be able to mark in a box which video(s) they watched. In another box on the poster, they will be able to mark on a scale of 0-5 how much it motivated them to tinker or discover the solution proposed in each of the videos they watched.

Materials

2 mailboxes, papers, stickers.
