How to crowdfund community resources?

Organize a collection to keep the community internet running.

Hi, I’m Sanjay, and one of my big passions is putting the resources we have to community use. I like tinkering, sure, but I’m also interested in how we humans coordinate to live better together.

What does it mean to run a collection for community internet?

This solution gathers ideas on how to raise money to pay for a community connection—in other words, getting a community to agree on financially supporting a resource that will be used and enjoyed by everyone.

What’s it for?

Finding ways to organize ourselves to acquire things that improve our living conditions is sometimes a complex task.

If you’re clear that you need the Internet, but don’t know how to get your community on board, get them to back the maintenance and sustainability of the contract, or you don’t know the steps to take to do it together, you can find some useful guidance in this solution.

What do you need?

  • A very clear idea of why you want to raise the money.
  • Charisma, a way with words, and people skills to convince folks to join the collection.
  • Organization to keep the books, manage the money, and keep your contributors updated on how the collection is going and the status of the accounts.
  • Communication skills to keep collaborators informed about the service status, maintenance needs, or any issues. Also, to receive suggestions or handle the paperwork required for formalizing or maintaining the service.

How to do it?

These recommendations are relevant when running money collections for community purposes. They don’t necessarily have to be followed in order, nor are they the only ones, but we believe they can help you.

Step 1 → Moment 1: Let’s build a team; it’s easier with others

Look for partners to start this campaign.

When we undertake projects for the common good, it’s easy to find allies to share responsibilities. When you do these kinds of initiatives alone, you can quickly get tired and give up, but with others, this project will be much more manageable.

Define who will handle what; that will make the work more organized and allow you to follow up more easily.

Step 2 → Let’s give the collection a purpose, something clear that gets us excited

It’s important to take the time to reflect and think about what we want to do. What is it that we want to achieve? Remember, money is just a means to reach something bigger and more important… that’s called purpose.

In this case, what is it that unites us in this collection, that inspires us to move to get the funds? When you are establishing the purpose of any project, it’s time to tirelessly ask ourselves why, until we are convinced.

Doing SOLE is a great way to find a purpose for the collection. Besides paying for it together, you can learn, talk, and create flourishing communities by using the internet as a group. Paying for the internet together allows you to use it for SOLE, or for anything else you can think of!

For example:

  • Why do we want internet in a public access space? So that people in my community can learn new things they need or want to learn, converse and dialogue with other people and communities in a Great Conversation, and take action in a self-organized way to live better together.
  • Why do we want people in our community to be able to learn together? So that it’s not necessary to depend exclusively on learning spaces and people related to the educational system, to realize that anyone can learn anything using the internet. Why in a group? Because going anywhere alone is a difficult path; instead, when we do it together, it’s better.
  • Why do we want them to be able to converse through the internet? Interacting with other people with the goal of learning or letting others be part of our lives always makes us feel better. Learning together when there are other people who participate in our findings and with whom we can walk the path together makes us feel that what we learn transcends and is bigger than us. If we connect with each other through the internet, it’s because we are mutually interested and can accompany each other; we are not alone on the path… Do you want to connect with people around the world in your SOLE sessions? Take a look at La Nube de Abuelitas SOLE! They are volunteers from all over the world who connect to SOLE sessions to accompany, offer warm encouragement, share, learn, and have fun together.
  • Why do we want to help our community flourish? So that we all have a happier, fuller, and more enjoyable life!

Does it make sense to have public access internet in our community? Absolutely!

Step 3 → Let’s identify potential people interested in participating

Which of our neighbors would be interested in having community internet? What interest might they have in contributing money to this project? This way, we can translate our project into their potential interests.

Here are some examples of SOLEros who have self-organized to learn together using the internet:

  • Elainer, when she used SOLE so that the young people in her community could learn to make video games.
  • Robinson, who used SOLE so that his community could find sustainable alternatives to their traditional crops.
  • María, when, through SOLE, she got her students to take responsibility for their own educational process and changed the way they research and learn at their school.

Step 4 → A story, the best way to communicate an idea

Now that we know who we are going to direct our campaign to, let’s create a story. We recommend answering five fundamental questions: who, what, when, where, and why.

Here is ours:

In the community of La Piche, delicious honey is produced. Everyone in the community works in trades related to the production of this delicious honey: some have hives, others collect the honey, others sell it, some make related products…

We are a group of young people interested in creating natural cosmetic products based on honey and beeswax, but we don’t know how to start making those products, nor the tools we need, nor the permits we require, or how we could market them, etc. We want to start with: how can we make a honey and beeswax lip balm?

Using the Internet, we could find answers to these and other Great Questions!

The cheapest Internet plan is 10,000 COP and put the fiber optic broadband internet in a place with easy access for everyone, such as the nursing home, the community hall, or Doña Adela’s shop, which is the business that is open most of the time and where we could all connect. We could all use the connection, even the children in the village, to do their homework.

So, if you are interested and can be part of this collection, join this list! Soon we will be able to have internet for ourselves!

I will send all expenses and receipt payments on the first of each month to a WhatsApp group, and we can keep up with any news there.

Are you in?

Step 5 → Buy, communicate, and organize expenses

With the money collected, you just have to make the purchase.

Remember that none of these contracts have long-term lock-in clauses, so if you are not satisfied with the service, you can cancel it and switch to another operator.

It is important to be organized with the accounts and communicate payments and any news or issues immediately to everyone involved.

This way, everyone can be co-responsible for any decision regarding this collection and can assume the responsibilities or consequences together.

Make sure to create a communication flow with your collaborators so you can communicate in both directions and quickly solve any problems that may arise.

How do you know if this solution works?

With this solution, you manage to organize neighbors toward a common good, embodied in the payment of a service, to take advantage of it collectively and get the most out of it.

You also manage to improve the connectivity conditions of the place where we live, making it a place where life is better.

Other possible Internets

Organizing ourselves to get things done will take us as far as we want to go.

In this case, it allows us to have a shared internet public access point. It is important to measure the bandwidth offered by the service to be contracted very well. This way, we will be able to contract a service that is fast and powerful enough in relation to the number of people we want to link to that connection.

The next step would be how to improve the conditions of this connectivity without a significant increase in costs.
