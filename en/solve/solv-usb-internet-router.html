Is a USB modem the best bet for me?

Check if a USB modem is the right fix to get your gear online.

Hi there! I’m Catalina, welcome 👋🏽. The first version of this solution was written by a colleague, Nicolás, and I’ve polished it up so we can navigate it together.

Are you wondering if a USB modem is the right way to get your computer online? Let’s figure it out together!

What is a USB modem?

It’s a compact device, shaped like a USB flash drive, that lets you insert a SIM card (just like the one in your phone) to give your computer an Internet connection.

What’s it for?

It lets you get online on devices that don’t have access to WiFi or wired (Ethernet) connections by tapping into mobile network signals. Plus, once it’s plugged in, your computer can become a Wi-Fi hotspot! This means you can share the signal with others, making it a great community or collective alternative to cut down on costs.

Do I really need one?

Before you buy, hold your horses for a second. Is this actually the best option for your situation?

  • Do you have a stable mobile signal where you are? If the answer is no, this solution won’t work well.
  • Does anyone in the community already have a device they could lend you or share?
  • Have you considered other alternatives? Sometimes, sharing data from a phone (tethering) might be enough without buying extra hardware.
  • Is the monthly budget for a data plan sustainable for you or your community?

How to do it

If, after weighing your options, you decide this is what you need, follow these steps:

Step 1 → Choose the carrier with the best signal

Not all carriers have the same signal strength in every area. Ask your neighbors which network works best for them or run a speed test with different SIM cards if you can get your hands on them.

Step 2 → Get the device

You can look for one directly through your trusted carrier or on online marketplaces like Mercado Libre. Search for “USB WiFi modem.” Make sure the device is “unlocked” so you can switch carriers if the signal is spotty.

Step 3 → Initial setup

  1. Insert the SIM card into the modem.
  2. Plug it into your computer’s USB port.
  3. If the device doesn’t install itself, look for the software that usually pops up in an internal folder when you open the USB drive in your file manager.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Don’t get fooled

The market often sells these USBs as “unlimited Internet.” There is no such thing as truly unlimited plans. Almost always, after a certain amount of gigabytes, the speed drops drastically. Read the “fine print” of the plan contract carefully before you pay.

How much is enough?

You don’t need top-tier speed to browse, look up information, or read the news. If the usage is shared, a moderate plan can be enough for everyone if used responsibly.
