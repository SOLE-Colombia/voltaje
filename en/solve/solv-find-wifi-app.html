What is scanning for nearby WiFi signals with a mobile app?

Scanning for WiFi signals → with a mobile app means using software on your smartphone to scan, analyze, and find wireless networks available right where you are. Often, you might think you don’t have Internet → or that your mobile signal is weak (which depends on the service your provider offers in your area), but if you look closely, there might be a WiFi network with a better connection or even a free one nearby.

What is it for?

Sometimes, the place where we are might not have access to Internet, but if we move around and head to a nearby spot, we might just find some WiFi networks. Even if we find them, we sometimes can’t access them because we don’t have the passwords.

The WiFi Map app shows us a map with WiFi networks within a 2-kilometer radius, based on your device’s location, and shares the access passwords with us. The networks saved in this app can be public or private.

If you want to make the most of a nearby connection, you can connect to those networks to use them as a group using SOLE. You only need a few devices, four or five people eager to learn per device, and Big Questions to solve. Want to know more about how to do a SOLE? Check out how to do it here: How to do a SOLE? - Tutorial

What do you need?

  • A Smartphone →
  • Available storage space on your phone
  • A mobile data connection
  • A bit of generosity

How do you do it?

Step 1 → Install the app

  • Before starting the installation process, you need to have a stable Internet connection.
  • Once you’ve got that, open the “Google Play” store for Android or the Apple Store for iOS and search for the WiFi Map app.
  • Another option is to click on one of these links using a web browser: Google Play and Apple Store

Step 2 → Set up the app

Once the app is installed, open it and accept the terms of use. It is very important that you grant it access to your location while the app is in use.

Open the app and swipe down from the top of your phone to access the control panel. Turn on your location if the app hasn’t already done so.

Step 3 → Use the app

When it shows you a map of the world, tap the “arrowhead” icon, and the map will center on your current region.

At the bottom of the screen, the closest WiFi networks for which we have the access password will appear.


Step 4 → Select a network

  • When you can see the list of WiFi networks nearby, you can select one to see its access details.
  • Sometimes the app will ask you to watch a video before letting you see the network passwords. This is how the app uses ads to fund itself in its free version.

How do you know if this solution works?

With this recipe, you can access multiple WiFi networks, both public and private. In cities or urban centers, you’ll have many more chances to find networks with available access data compared to smaller, more remote towns.

Now, with a WiFi network, you’ll have more stability and speed to connect to the Internet—and why not use it to find answers to your Big Questions, learn something new, take courses, or share the resource with others?

Are you ready to get started?

Other possible Internets

This solution will allow you to have temporary Internet access, mainly in towns where users like you have shared access data for public or private Internet networks.

To access these passwords, you must have an internet connection, as only the paid version of the app allows you to download the map with the access information.
