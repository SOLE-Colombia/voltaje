How to buy a temporary satellite internet connection?

A code or PIN to access satellite internet.

A code or PIN to access satellite internet

Hi! I’m Belén. I didn’t write this entire solution, but while reviewing it, I remembered many SOLE sessions that work thanks to this workaround. Finding a place that sells a few data or minutes of satellite internet connection has allowed us to bring SOLE to places we never thought we’d reach.

What is getting an Internet PIN?

Accessing the internet via a satellite connection is a recommended method in places where cables or phone lines don’t reach, such as rural or remote areas. In a city, it serves as an alternative and somewhat affordable system compared to the usual ones.

In Colombia, a “satellite connection PIN” refers to a code or key that allows access to internet or communication services via satellites. These codes might be needed to activate a satellite internet service, like Starlink, or to configure satellite communication devices used in areas without terrestrial network coverage.

This solution explains how to access satellite internet as a pay-as-you-go service by the day or hour.

What is it for?

If you’re looking to use an internet connection for something specific, this is the ideal solution. For example, if you want to gather your community to learn things together using the internet as a group—that is, to hold a SOLE—having data to share across two or three computers so everyone can ask the web about the things they want to learn is the best way to guarantee a connection.

What do you need?

  • An internet provider in the area.
  • Money to pay for the PIN.
  • A device to access the internet.

How to do it?

Follow these steps to buy the PIN and get connected:

Step 1 → Check if this service exists in your area

First of all, you need to know if this service is regularly offered in your territory. This service exists in places where mobile data networks from conventional providers don’t reach; if they did, it would be more practical to just top up your phone with a data package or have a postpaid plan.

In some places, internet providers lease satellite connections where cable can’t reach or where they know locals can’t contract other types of mobile phone services. For example, here is a company that offers this type of service in Barranquilla: Zonawipi


Step 2 → Evaluate other options before deciding

If you found that the service is available in your area, it’s important to see if it’s economically convenient for you. If you have to keep buying PINs and traveling to another location every day to connect, this might not be the most viable option. Perhaps another solution would be better, like pooling money together to pay for community internet.

If it is definitely the most viable option, go ahead and buy it!


Step 3 → Buy the PIN and start browsing

Now that you’ve decided to buy a PIN, remember to make the most of it. Connection time will be restricted only to the areas where this network has coverage. In practice, it works like an Internet Café where you connect wirelessly with your own device.

Just follow the instructions on the paper where the purchased PIN comes. Usually, you need to connect to a WiFi network, which will automatically redirect you to a link asking for the PIN number to grant you internet access. That’s it—now get connected and enjoy!

Remember, you’ll get great value out of your internet connection if you share it with others who can’t access this resource. A great way to learn and enjoy together is to hold a SOLE. SOLE is a methodology where real-life challenges can be solved through Big Questions using the internet. Does your community want to learn how to get better results from their crops? Do you want to know what games children in other countries play? Do you have questions about how a specific atmospheric phenomenon is produced or how to take advantage of climate changes? Hold a SOLE! You can find more information at Sole Colombia.

How do you know this solution works?

With this solution, you can achieve a temporary connection with internet access in a specific location.

Why might this solution work?

We have to carefully measure the bandwidth offered by the service and, in general, the quality of the service used in relation to its cost. This solution is intended for non-recurring access, as it would become very expensive if it were to become a regular habit, and it would end up being cheaper to contract other types of services.
