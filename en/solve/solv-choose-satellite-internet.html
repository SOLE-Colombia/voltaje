¿Qué Internet satelital elegir según mis necesidades?

Invierte bien tu dinero para instalar internet satelital.

Hi, I’m Nicolás, and just like you, I’ve been learning how to improve my internet connection. I’m no expert, but by searching around, following tutorials, and learning through trial and error, I’ve managed to improve internet quality in places that don’t have great connectivity.

In this specific solution, we’ll figure out the criteria for choosing which satellite internet best fits our connectivity needs. We’ll also face some ethical questions that might influence our choice.

Our advice: decide with both your head and your heart—choose the option that best serves your needs and those of your community.

What does it mean to choose satellite internet with criteria?

Satellite internet is a technology designed to send an internet signal from a satellite in orbit to a satellite dish at your home or business.

With satellite internet, you can connect computers, smartphones →, tablets, and more. This technology doesn’t require the cabling used by, for example, fiber-optic internet.

What is it for?

This solution is here to help you decide which satellite internet is worth buying if you’re located in a place without an internet connection.

In this guide, we’ll cover the pros and cons of three different solutions so you can make a conscious and responsible decision: satellite dish, WiMax, or Starlink.

Before you start, we suggest that if you decide to buy any of the options presented here, you try to split the cost with others so you can enjoy the internet as a community.

Before we begin, some ethical questions

Do you believe that everyone, regardless of where they are, should have access to the internet? In Colombia, for example, a 2021 legislative act established and elevated the internet to a fundamental right, making it an obligation to expand internet coverage to unconnected communities and territories. Meanwhile, international organizations like the UN state that denying internet access is like denying the right to freedom of expression.

This might sound great, but the reality is complex and unequal. On one hand, not everyone has internet: nearly a third of the world’s population remains offline—about 2.6 billion people. Internet service providers aren’t keen on reaching places without infrastructure because the potential for profit is lower. This widens and maintains the inequity in internet access. On the other hand, the deployment of thousands of satellites in low Earth orbit has raised concerns about environmental impact and the creation of space debris.

So, at SOLE Colombia, we see satellite internet as one of the available options, specifically designed for places that don’t have an internet connection or nearby telecommunications towers to distribute it. If that’s not your case, you might find a more conscious solution tailored to your needs elsewhere in this guide.

How do I know if I need this solution?

Before we start, we’d like to ask you a few questions:

  • Do you currently have fiber-optic or mobile internet of fair or good quality?
  • Where you live, can you see telecommunications towers that distribute internet?
  • Are there companies that can offer you an internet service at a reasonable cost?

If you answered “yes” to any of these questions, we recommend sticking with your mobile or fiber-optic internet and looking for other solutions on SOLE Voltaje → that might help you improve your connection. But don’t go yet! We invite you to keep reading this guide so you can learn more about satellite internet and make your own informed decisions.

If, on the other hand, you answered “no” to all the questions above, you have a poor internet connection, and you’ve already checked out other solutions, we invite you to keep reading.

Options to review

In this solution, we will look at three technologies available in Colombia as of 2024: Satellite Internet, WiMax, and Starlink. The estimated reading time for this solution is about 15 minutes, but don’t be intimidated—you can read these recommendations with the help of others. Remember, at SOLE Voltaje, we want to promote the collective use of the internet.

  • Satellite Internet The most popular option for internet in rural areas is satellite internet. Although the name is self-explanatory, it involves connecting to the internet via satellites. The great advantage of this option is that it can reach anywhere, including the most remote rural areas! To access this technology, the company will need to install a satellite dish at your home.

  • WiMax How WiMAX works is simple: it acts as a repeater for the company you hire, emitting microwave signals that reach your antenna. Therefore, the first thing you need to do is contact a company that offers these services and has a repeater near your home to send you the signal. Then, you have to mount an antenna on the facade of your house. This antenna must point directly at the service provider’s repeater or at an intermediate connection point used to bypass obstacles between your antenna and their repeater. The company itself will handle the antenna installation.

_Image taken from Wikipedia

  • Starlink Starlink is Elon Musk’s satellite internet project. It is a private satellite internet service that facilitates connections, especially in rural areas. The company is considered the world’s first and largest satellite constellation using a low Earth orbit to offer broadband satellite internet with high speed and low latency. This allows for streaming content, online gaming, video calls, and more.

To get it running, the user buys a kit with everything needed, most notably the antenna, which must be placed outdoors. It automatically orients itself to establish a connection in a matter of minutes, provided it has a clear view of the sky.

_Image taken from Starlink

Now that we have an introduction to the available options, let’s get to the part where you learn the criteria you should keep in mind to choose the option that suits you best.

What should I keep in mind to make this decision?

Availability: Are these options available where I live?

Although we conducted this analysis in 2024 for Colombia, it may be useful for other countries. To know the availability of these three options, keep in mind that, unlike WiMax, Satellite Internet and Starlink have global coverage, so they don’t depend exclusively on local providers. For the WiMax option, the first thing you must do is contact the companies offering internet services in your region; they will be able to guide you and tell you if they actually offer this technology.

Budget: How much can I afford?

  • The cost of WiMax service can start from 300,000 COP and $2,000,000 COP, depending on the company you choose.
  • The cost of satellite internet service can start from 300,000 COP and $2,000,000 COP, depending on the company you choose.
  • The cost of Starlink service can start from 1,900,000 COP.

Weather conditions: Can the weather affect the internet signal?

  • For Starlink and Satellite Internet, the quality of the internet will depend on weather conditions. Using a satellite dish requires a clear sky; for this reason, signal stability could be compromised by rain or thunderstorms.
  • For WiMax, keep in mind that a direct line of sight to the repeater is necessary. This means the antenna you install at home must be oriented directly toward the site sending the connection, ideally avoiding obstacles that block the line of sight, such as trees or buildings. Sometimes, it may be necessary to install intermediate connection points to bypass obstacles.

Signal and coverage: Which option offers the best quality?

  • The advantage of Satellite Internet is that it has global coverage, meaning people living in remote regions or rural areas can access the internet reliably. One of the most notable advantages of satellite internet is its ability to provide a constant and stable connection. Unlike terrestrial connections, which can be affected by infrastructure interruptions or adverse weather conditions, satellite internet doesn’t rely on cables. This ensures a reliable and continuous connection, even in areas prone to natural disasters.
  • However, the downside of satellite internet is that many providers offer a limited amount of data that can be downloaded and uploaded over a certain period. If this limit is exceeded, speeds may be throttled, or additional charges may apply.
  • For WiMax, the advantage is that it doesn’t require a residential telephone service. Having a landline is not a requirement to access the internet service.
  • On the downside, the download speed will be much higher than the upload speed. WiMAX connections can reach speeds of up to 1Gbps at fixed points (like a house) and 365Mbps at mobile points. Even so, it will always depend on what the company you hire offers. Also, keep in mind that speed could be more easily affected during peak hours.
  • For its part, Starlink offers an easy installation that you and your community can carry out: the company provides the user with a kit including the necessary instructions for assembly. Starlink also offers flexibility and mobility. You can have internet access from anywhere covered by the satellite signal, making it an ideal option for people who travel frequently, remote workers, or those who need connectivity in temporary or expanding areas.
  • However, since the Starlink system relies on satellites in low orbit, capacity is limited and shared among users in a given area. In congested areas, this could result in reduced speeds during peak hours, although its coverage is constantly improving. Some reports suggest that Starlink can experience interference issues, especially in densely populated areas or regions with high satellite activity. For this reason, keep in mind that using Starlink requires a direct view of the sky, which may limit its viability in areas with significant obstructions like tall trees or buildings.

What signal speed do I need?

By latency →, we refer to the time it takes for the signal to travel from the satellite to Earth. For Starlink, the broadband internet is high-speed with low latency. It will have latencies of around 20ms, compared to the average of 600ms.

For its part, WiMax allows for a fast and fluid connection with low latency.

However, the speed of satellite internet can be lower compared to terrestrial connections. This can affect real-time activities like video conferencing or online gaming, where even small delays can be noticeable.
